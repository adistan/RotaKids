ROTAKIDS GUIDE FOR ROTARY CLUB SPONSORS

1. Why Sponsor a RotaKids Club?

Sponsoring a RotaKids club is a strategic investment in the future of Rotary.

-  Member Pipeline: Children who begin at age 6–7 become dedicated  Interactors by

age 12-13.

-  Community Visibility: RotaKids projects attract the attention of parents, local media,

and public authorities.

-  Club  Revitalization:  Interaction  with  the  enthusiasm  of  children  brings  renewed

energy to Rotary members.

2. Choosing an Organizational Model

RotaKids sponsorship may be organized by one Rotary Club or by two or more co-sponsoring

Rotary Clubs, subject to District approval. A single Rotary Club may sponsor more than one

RotaKids club, especially where several schools or community groups are active in the same

locality.  Similarly,  several  Rotary  Clubs  may  jointly  sponsor  one  or  more  RotaKids  clubs

where this provides stronger mentorship, continuity, funding, or geographic coverage. In all

co-sponsorship  models,  one  lead  sponsoring  Rotary  Club  should  be  designated  for

certification, reporting, safeguarding coordination, financial oversight, and communication

with the District.

Depending on your club's resources, you may choose between two structures:

A. School-Based Model - Most Sustainable

-  Partnership: The sponsoring Rotary Club, or a group of co-sponsoring Rotary Clubs,

collaborates with a local primary school.

-  Location: Meetings are held on school premises.

-  Leadership: A volunteer teacher (Faculty Advisor) manages day-to-day activities.

-  Key  Advantage:  Day-to-day  child  protection  procedures  are  administered  through

the school's existing protocols, under a formal agreement with the sponsoring Rotary

Club.  The  Rotary  Club  provides  mentorship,  vision,  and  oversight  that  appropriate

safeguards are in place.

B. Community-Based Model - Most Flexible

-  Partnership: The sponsoring Rotary Club, or a group of co-sponsoring Rotary Clubs,

organizes  the  group  independently  (e.g.,  for  children  of  members  or  from  the  local

neighborhood).

-  Location: The Rotary Club premises or community spaces.

-  Leadership: A Rotary member (Rotarian Advisor) coordinates the group.

3. Steps to Launch (The 5-Step Launch)

1.  Appoint a Committee: Designate a "RotaKids Chairman" within the sponsoring Rotary

Club. In a co-sponsorship model, the co-sponsoring Rotary Clubs should  designate

one lead sponsoring Rotary Club and agree on a common coordination structure. A

long-term mandate is recommended for continuity.

2.  Identify a Partner: Contact a school administration or organize a community group.
3.  Information Session: Present the RotaKids values and Pledge to parents and children.
4.  Charter  Ceremony:  The  District  issues  the  Official  Charter.  The  first  badges  are

presented (Solid Wheel - Level I).

5.  Register  on  RotaKids.net:  Add  your  club  to  the  world  map  to  be  visible  and  to

collaborate with other clubs.

4. Mentorship and the Merit System (The Gear of Service)

The role of the sponsoring club is to guide children through the 3 Levels and 9 milestones of

the Gear of Service:

-  Encouragement:  Attend  at  least  one  meeting  per  month,  providing  continuity  of

Rotarian presence and mentorship throughout the member's journey.

-  Recognition: Organize formal Wheel Loading Ceremonies for the progressive filling

of circle markers on the children's badges, marking each milestone achieved through

verified volunteer service.

-  Transfer: At the appropriate age, ensure a formal Graduation Ceremony marking the

transition  from  RotaKids  to  the  local  Interact  club,  presenting  the  RotaKids

Programme  Graduation  Certificate  and  facilitating  continuity  within  the  Rotary

family.

5. Funding: The Zero-Fee Model

RotaKids is designed to be accessible to everyone:

-  No fees to RI and no mandatory child dues: All resources remain at the local level,

and no child may be required or expected to contribute financially.

-  Self-Funding:  Teach  children  to  organize  small  fundraising  projects  (lemonade

stands, handmade craft fairs).

-  Sponsor  Support:  The  Rotary  Club  is  responsible  for  covering  the  cost  of  member

badges (under USD 0.50 per unit). Optionally, the Rotary Club may also contribute to

the  cost  of  a  standard  RotaKids  uniform  (typically  a  cap  and  a  T-shirt)  or  other

promotional materials, at its own discretion and according to available resources.

6. Digital Resources

The  RotaKids  digital  infrastructure  is  offered  as  a  foundational  asset  developed  by  the

pioneers of District 2241. Its current components have different levels of maturity and are

subject to RI-approved technical, privacy, safeguarding, and governance review before any

official deployment.

-  ROTAKIDS.ORG:  Public  documentation  and  resolution-support  website,

including

governance documents, templates, and supporting materials.

-  ROTAKIDS.NET:  World  club  registry,  currently  maintained  manually  by  the  current

administrator, based on identified and verified clubs.

- ROTAKIDS.COM: PHP/HTML prototype reserved for future project, fundraising, or impact-

related tools; not an operational MVP and not an official fundraising platform.

-  ROTAKIDS.CLUB:  Basic  reserved  presence  for  future  club-level  digital  tools;  not  yet  an

operational platform.
