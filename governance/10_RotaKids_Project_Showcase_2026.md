RotaKids in Action

A Global Portfolio of Service Projects

ANNEX B

This  annex  documents  verified  service  projects  carried  out  by  RotaKids  clubs  and,  where

explicitly  identified,  comparable  pre-Interact  Rotary-family  youth  programmes  such  as

EarlyAct and FuturAct, across more than 15 countries between 2010 and 2026. Projects are

organized  by  theme.  Each  entry  reflects  real  activity  documented  through  Rotary  district

websites, school publications, and local press. Entries identified as EarlyAct, FuturAct, or other

analogous  programmes  are  included  as  comparative  evidence  of  Rotary-family  service-

learning models for children under Interact age, not as RotaKids clubs.

cones  into  a  handcrafted  Christmas  tree,  which  was  auctioned  at  the  Rotary  Club  of

Pitești charity ball in December 2024, with proceeds supporting community projects. The

club also participated in the annual Rotary District 2241 environmental month (March),

raising awareness about clean surroundings and recycling among their school peers.

Planet”, an  inter-club  environmental awareness event gathering children aged  3 to 13.

Participants  competed  in  painting  and  fancy  dress  across  themes  including  water

conservation, wildlife protection, renewable energy, and pollution control. The event was

described by its organizers as "a celebration of young voices committed to change."

Rotary's annual focus areas. In March - the month dedicated to environmental protection

educational activities about water conservation and recycling.

clean-up days alongside adult Rotarians. The club also runs a vision-testing programme

and  dental  health  initiatives  at  the  school,  connecting  environmental  awareness  with

broader community health education.

CATEGORY 3: ELDERLY, DISABLED & VULNERABLE COMMUNITY SUPPORT

21. Quilters Junior School RotaKids - Billericay, Essex, United Kingdom (District 1240) Since

pandemic  by  letter,  earned  the  school  the  first  Dementia  Friendly  Schools  award  in

Billericay, and culminated in an annual Tea Party where elderly pen pals visit the school

for  games,  an  intergenerational  choir  performance,  and  conversation.  The  club  also

attends  HumankindCafe  to  sing  and  do  crafts  with  isolated  community  members.  The

Rotary  Club  of  Billericay  contributed  £320  toward  postage  costs.  Both  Quilters  and  its

partner  Buttsbury  Junior  School  have  received  Presidential  Citations  from  Rotary

International - among the very few RotaKids clubs globally to receive this distinction.

culture,  embedded  since  the  1996  founding  of  the  programme,  remains  active  across

hundreds of Brazilian RotaKids clubs today.

your own school gate to children on the other side of the world who may need comfort

43. Rotary India Literacy Mission - TEACH Programme Schools, India (Multiple Districts)

The Rotary India Literacy Mission (RILM), operating through Rotary clubs across 15 Indian

states,  has  transformed  over  27,000  government  schools  into  Happy  Schools  and

enrolled more than 50,000 out-of-school children back into education through its Asha

Kiran  programme.  In  partnership  with  NCERT  and  India's  Ministry  of  Education,  RILM

produced free e-learning content reaching 420 million users through national television

and  mobile  platforms.  Rotary's  youngest  members  -  Interactors  and  early-age

participants - serve as peer educators using the "Each One, Teach One" model to bring

basic literacy to adults in their families and communities.

EarlyAct programme run structured literacy projects aligned with their three mandatory

annual  service  areas:  school,  local  community,  and  international  understanding.  Local

literacy activities include collecting books for under-resourced classrooms, participating

in  school  reading  events,  and  creating  awareness  campaigns  about  the  value  of

education. The programme's character education mission explicitly connects literacy to

active citizenship - one of Rotary's core global commitments.

Guanajuato  state,  where  educational  access  disparities  between  urban  and  rural

communities remain significant, making children's awareness of education as a right - not

a privilege - a core part of the club's civic formation agenda.
