1. Statement of Principles

Rotary  International  is committed  to creating  and  maintaining  a  safe  environment  for  all

young people participating in its activities. For the RotaKids segment - children aged 7 to 12,

with  a  one-year  extension  in  either  direction permitted  upon  approval  of  the  sponsoring

Rotary Club and in accordance with applicable local law - safety is the absolute priority. This

framework ensures compliance with the national legislation specific to each jurisdiction.

2. The Decentralized Accountability Model (The Shield)

To eliminate legal risks at the global level (Evanston), the RotaKids program operates under

the following delegation of responsibility:

-  RI's Role: Provides brand authorization (Turquoise, PMS 7466C / Hex #00adbb), the

educational framework, and general ethical standards.

-  The  District's  Role:  Is  the  legal  authority  responsible  for  implementing  and

monitoring compliance with local child protection laws (e.g., GDPR in the EU, COPPA

in the USA).

-  The Sponsor Club's Role: Directly executes safety protocols at every meeting or event.

3. Safety Protocols by Organizational Model

A. School-Based Model

-  Delegation of Responsibility: Legal responsibility for the physical and digital safety of

minors rests with the educational institution.

-  Supervision: At least one Faculty Advisor must be present at every RotaKids activity.

-  Standards:  The  child  protection  policy  of  the  respective  school  applies,  already

approved by the Ministry of Education of the host country.

B. Community-Based Model

-  Responsibility: Rests exclusively with the sponsoring Rotary Club.

-  Adult Screening: Any Rotarian who interacts directly with RotaKids members must

present  a  valid  criminal  background  check  certificate  and  complete  the  volunteer

screening procedure established by the District.

-  The Golden Rule (2-Adult Rule): No adult may be alone with a RotaKid at any time.

Every activity requires the presence of at least two verified adults.

generic  project  imagery  if  explicit  parental  consent  for  online  publication  has  not

been secured.

5. Incident Procedure

Any suspected breach of child safety must be reported within 24 hours to:

1.  The District Youth Protection Officer.
2.  The relevant local legal authorities.
3.  The District Governor.

Note: RI receives only annual statistical compliance reports - not the details of individual cases -

in order to avoid direct legal liability.

6. Mentor Identification

The official Turquoise color (PMS 7466C / Hex #00adbb) shall be used on all mentor badges

and safety materials to visually identify adult persons who have completed the verification

process (background check).
