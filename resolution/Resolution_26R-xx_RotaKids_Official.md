RESOLUTION 26R-xx
Recognition of "RotaKids" as an Authorized District-Led Rotary Family Youth Program

Proposer(s): Rotary Club of Pitesti, District 2241, Romania & Republic of Moldova

RESOLUTION

The  Council  on  Resolutions  requests  the  RI  Board  to  consider  officially  recognizing

"RotaKids" as an authorized district-led Rotary family youth program, authorizing the use of

the RotaKids brand and standardized logo, establishing that legal, financial, safeguarding,

and  operational  responsibility  rests  with  the  sponsoring  Rotary  Club  and  District  in

accordance  with  local

law,  and  considering  the  transfer,  licensing,  or  designated

stewardship  of  the  global  digital

infrastructure

(rotakids.com/.net/.org/.club  and

rotarykids.com/.net/.org)  offered  by  the  founding  pioneers  of  District  2241  to  serve  as  a

foundational  digital  asset  for  the  programme's  future  global  hub,  subject  to  RI-approved

technical, privacy, safeguarding, and governance review.

Proposer's Purpose and Effect Statement

Small Hands. Big Change.

This resolution seeks to bridge the gap between the grassroots success of RotaKids and the

need for a unified identity. By clarifying that responsibility remains at the District level, we

address Rotary International’s primary concern regarding legal liability. This allows Rotary

International  to  inspire  youth  without  the  administrative  burden  of  a  centrally  managed

"Structured Program" like Interact, while creating a clear pathway for protecting the brand

and making the existing global domains available under terms acceptable to RI.

This  recognition  also  ensures  that  the  program's  continued  growth,  already  documented

across  verified  clubs  in  multiple  regions  worldwide,  benefits  from  the  credibility  and

consistency that only official Rotary recognition can provide.

The  complete  governance  framework  supporting  this  resolution,  including  the  Standard

RotaKids Club Constitution, Standard Bylaws, Statement of Policy, Safeguarding Framework,

and supporting documentation, is publicly available at rotakids.org/resolution/

This  resolution  aligns  directly  with  the  2026-2027  Rotary  Presidential  Message,  Create

Lasting Impact. Recognizing RotaKids is an investment in the most lasting impact Rotary can

make: shaping the next generation of service-oriented leaders from their earliest years.

Significant Financial Impact

No substantial financial impact anticipated for Rotary International.
